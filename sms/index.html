SMS Switchboard

Reply to voter texts and send message blasts. Sign in with your campaign (phone-bank) account.

💬 Open the Switchboard Reply queue & campaign blasts — sign-in required 🧰 All campaign tools Phone bank, broadcast, sign-ups & more

How it works

  1. Sign in. Use your phone-bank username and password — same account for both tools.
  2. Reply Queue. Answers the oldest unanswered text first. Type a reply, hit Send & next. Inbound photos (MMS) show inline.
  3. Blast. Pick or create a campaign, write a message (use {Name}), optionally attach an image, and send to pending rows. Use Import volunteers to pull a list straight from the master sheet.
Compliance: keep "Reply STOP to opt out" in every blast (CASL). STOP only blocks texts — it does not block phone calls.
