How it works

  1. Sign in. Open the tool and enter the staff password.
  2. Pick / build a campaign. Choose a recorded message and the list of numbers to call from the control sheet.
  3. Launch. Everyone except DNC is dialed. Results and any "press 1" responses are logged back to the sheet.
Heads up: DNC numbers are skipped automatically and stay in sync with the other calling tools (hourly DNC sync). Opt-outs collected here also feed that shared list.
