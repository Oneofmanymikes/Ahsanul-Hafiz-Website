Check my ID →

The short version. It depends on one thing: whether you got a voter information card in the mail.

If you have your voter information card: bring it, plus any one piece of ID showing your name. A health card, passport, credit or debit card, student card, union card, even a blood donor card.
If you did not get one, or cannot find it: bring any one document showing your name and your current address. Just one, not two.

Plenty of ordinary things show name and address:

  • Driver’s licence, Ontario Photo Card or vehicle permit
  • Utility bill: hydro, water, gas, phone or cable TV
  • Bank account or credit card statement
  • Pay stub, cheque stub or T4 from your employer
  • Lease, rental or mortgage statement
  • Insurance statement
  • Income tax assessment, Child Tax Benefit, Old Age Security or EI statement
  • Tuition bill, transcript, report card or school admission letter
  • Property tax bill or MPAC assessment notice
  • CNIB card, or a long-term care home residence document
  • Letter of confirmation of residence
Do not let ID stop you from going. You do not need photo ID, and if you do show photo ID your appearance does not have to match it. An electronic copy on your phone is accepted. If you are not sure what you have, call us at 647-313-2506 and we will work it out with you. Permanent resident cards and international ID are not accepted.

Who can vote

You can vote if you are a Canadian citizen, 18 or older on September 3, and you live in Scarborough Southwest. You do not need to be registered in advance. You can register right at the polling place when you go to vote.

Need a ride, or have a question?

Our campaign will drive you to vote, free, on election day. If you cannot get there on your own, tell us and we will sort it out.
