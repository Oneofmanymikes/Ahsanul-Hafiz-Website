    What to bring

    The simplest answer is your driver's licence. It shows your name and your address together, so on its own it is everything you need.

    No licence? Bring something else that shows your name and current address:

    • Ontario Photo Card
    • Ontario Motor Vehicle Permit
    • A utility, phone or cable bill
    • Bank or credit card statement
    • Income tax assessment, Child Tax Benefit or Old Age Security statement
    • Most documents from the Government of Canada, the Government of Ontario, or a municipality

    If your name and address are already on the voters list, one piece of ID showing just your name can be enough, a health card or passport for example. Bring proof of your address as well if you can, though, so there is nothing to sort out at the door.

    Do not let ID stop you from going. You do not need photo ID, and if you do show photo ID your appearance does not have to match it. An electronic copy on your phone is accepted. If you turn up without ID there is still a way to vote, so go anyway and ask the staff. Permanent resident cards and international ID are not accepted.

    Bring your voter information card if you received one in the mail. It makes things faster, but you can vote without it.

    Who can vote

    You can vote if you are a Canadian citizen, 18 or older on September 3, and you live in Scarborough Southwest. You do not need to be registered in advance. You can register right at the polling place when you go to vote.
