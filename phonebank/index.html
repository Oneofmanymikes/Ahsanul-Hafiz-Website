Thanks for helping reach voters! Pick an option below. Most volunteers want Start Calling.

📞 Start Calling For volunteers — make voter calls 📊 Operator Dashboard Staff only — password required

How it works (volunteers)

  1. Sign in. Open Start Calling, enter your name, and choose how you want to talk: computer audio (use a headset) or your phone (we call you and you stay on the line).
  2. Pick a community. Choose the cultural community you'll be calling from the dropdown.
  3. Press Start. Nothing dials until you're ready. When you press Start, the system finds a voter and connects them to you automatically — usually in a few seconds.
  4. Greet by name & follow the script. The voter's name is shown big at the top. If more than one person lives at that number, you'll see a card for each one.
  5. Record the results. For each person you speak with, tap their Support ID, volunteer interest, sign request, and any notes. Anyone you don't reach is saved as "Not Home."
  6. End & choose a disposition. Hit the red End button, pick what happened (Spoke / Voicemail / Wrong number / etc.) — this is required — then press Save & Next to get your next call.

Good to know

  • You control the pace — calls only come when you press Save & Next, so take your time writing notes.
  • Need a breather? Use Save & Break and you'll stop receiving calls until you resume.
  • If everyone in your community has been reached, you'll be prompted to switch to another community.
  • Missed-call voters who call the number back are forwarded to the campaign line automatically.
  • Use a quiet room and a headset if you can — it makes you much easier to hear.
Operators: the dashboard is password-protected. Use it to Start/Stop the run, watch the live funnel and per-community counts, monitor caller-ID health, and reset between practice runs. Full step-by-step instructions are on the dashboard itself.
