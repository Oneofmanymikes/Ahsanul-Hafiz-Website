Stand With Ontario’s Health Care Heroes

The nurses, PSWs, doctors, paramedics, and hospital staff who care for us are burning out — and being asked to do more with less. It’s time to stand with them.

Add your name below — be one of the first to sign.

Across Ontario — and here in Scarborough — the people who keep our hospitals and homes running face burnout, workplace violence, and years of disrespect. That pressure drives good people out of health care, and it puts every patient at risk. The conditions of work are the conditions of care.

The Health Care HEROES Act is the first serious plan in years to fix it — a real strategy to Honour, Employ, Retain, Optimize and Empower the health care workers we all depend on.

What the HEROES Act delivers
  • Fixes Bill 124 — finishes negotiations and delivers the retroactive pay health care workers are owed.
  • Fair pay & wage parity across home, community, long-term, primary and acute care.
  • Safer workplaces — real protection from violence, safe staffing levels, free secure parking, and proper breaks.
  • Mental-health support — OHIP-funded crisis lines and therapy built for health care workers.
  • Real recognition — a Health Professionals’ Week and new provincial honours for exceptional care.
  • Accountability — public reporting on staffing, with financial penalties on the Minister and Premier for missed deadlines.
We, the undersigned residents of Ontario, call on the provincial government to pass the Health Care HEROES Act and act now to honour, retain, and protect the health care workers we all depend on.
